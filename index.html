Where it starts

About

Most of my projects started as a question from somewhere else in my life: the batter's box, a market, a lab.

  • Baseball

    → decisions under uncertainty

    My first program tried to tell a hitter what to look for. That question became probabilistic modelling.

  • Markets

    → honest forecasting

    I build forecasting systems and hold them to a random-walk baseline, even when they lose.

  • Physics

    → computing with light

    What if the physics of a wave could compute its own gradient? I trained a block of glass to read digits.
